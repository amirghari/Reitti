# Photo credits

Downloaded and served from this origin. Never hot-linked: the design export loads
these from `images.unsplash.com`, and a request to a third party on every page load
would undercut the claim that nothing about a visit leaves the device.

## hero-2000.webp, hero-1000.webp, hero-1600.jpg

- **Scene:** an older person walking along a park path in autumn.
- **Photographer:** Victoria Prymak (@vprmk)
- **Photo:** https://unsplash.com/photos/gUeWxej5wzE
- **Licence:** Unsplash License, https://unsplash.com/license — the page states
  "Free to use under the Unsplash License".
- **Downloaded:** 2026-09-24, checked against the photo page the same day.
- **Processing:** cropped to landscape (`crop=bottom`, so the person and the path
  are in frame rather than the canopy) and re-encoded by the Unsplash CDN at
  download time. Nothing about the scene was altered.

## The second photo in the design was NOT used

The export also references `photo-1656426050682-148eb5e04c24`, "People on a rocky
shore by the sea", on the mobile landing frame. It is not here, and not shipped.

The export credits only one photo page, and that page is the autumn path above. The
rocky-shore image could not be traced to an Unsplash photo page, so its photographer
could not be named and its licence could not be read. The redesign task says to stop
rather than ship a photo whose licence page cannot be confirmed. The verified photo
is used for the hero instead, which also satisfies the task's rule that the directory
header may carry a photo only if it is the same one as the hero.
