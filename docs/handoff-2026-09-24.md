## The filter problem

**A library computer cannot open the site.** Reported 24.9: `ERR_CONNECTION_RESET` on a
Metropolia library machine.

What was ruled out the same day: HTTPS 200 over HTTP/2, TLS 1.3 with a valid certificate, the
http→https redirect, and DNS-level blocking. Quad9, Cloudflare's family resolver and **Cisco
Umbrella**, which powers a lot of school and library filtering, all resolve the domain to the
real IP. The domain carries no malicious or phishing reputation.

So it is the library's own appliance resetting the connection on the hostname in the TLS
handshake. The most likely cause is that **the domain was registered on 17.9.2026** and newly
registered domains are a standard blocking category with a roughly 30-day window, which closes
around **17.10.2026** by itself.

**Why this matters to you specifically.** Your targets are libraries, student services and
universities. A recipient who clicks your link from inside an institutional network may see a
broken site and conclude the project is broken. Nobody reports this; they just leave.

Three practical consequences:

1. **Expect silence that is not disinterest.** If a library or university contact goes quiet
   after a link, the link may simply have failed for them.
2. **Offer a fallback in those emails.** `https://reitti-seven.vercel.app` serves the same app
   on an older hostname. Be aware `*.vercel.app` is itself blocked on some networks, because
   phishing sites use it, so it is a fallback and not a guarantee.
3. **The fix needs a person.** The vendor categorisation forms (Talos, FortiGuard, Symantec,
   Zscaler, Palo Alto, Netcraft) all have captchas and refuse automated requests. Ten minutes of
   a human's time clears the block for every institution using that vendor. Ask for **Health &
   Medicine**.

Full detail and a drafted allowlist request for Metropolia IT and Helmet:
`docs/network-filter-block.md`. Logged as **B16**.
