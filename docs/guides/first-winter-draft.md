| Fact | Source | Date on the page |
|---|---|---|
| At the winter solstice the day in Helsinki lasts less than 6 hours | FMI, https://www.ilmatieteenlaitos.fi/tahtitieteelliset-vuodenajat | none shown |
| Kaamos (polar night) lasts almost two months in Nuorgam, 25.11 to 17.1; in Sodankylä four days before Christmas | FMI, same page | none shown |
| Vitamin D: 10 µg a day is the recommended intake for children and adults; 20 µg from age 75 | Ruokavirasto, https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/elintarvikkeet/terveytta-edistava-ruokavalio/ravintoaineet/d-vitamiini/ | updated 6.2.2026 |

| 20% of women and 11% of men report a lot of winter (kaamos) symptoms; most common at 20 to 39; unchanged 2017 to 2023 | THL, Terve Suomi survey: https://www.thl.fi/tervesuomi_verkkoraportit/ilmioraportit_2023/mieliala_ahdistuneisuus_ja_kaamosoireet.html | published 5.10.2023 |
